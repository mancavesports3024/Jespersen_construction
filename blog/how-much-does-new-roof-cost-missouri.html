How much does a new roof cost in Missouri?

No honest contractor should quote a firm price from a blog post—here is what actually moves the number.

Blog · Serving Joplin and Southwest Missouri.

Why “per square” talk is incomplete

Roofing is sold in 100-square-foot “squares,” but total cost depends on steepness, how many stories, how cut-up the roof is (valleys and hips add labor), and whether we are removing multiple layers. A simple ranch can be very different from a multi-plane two-story with dead valleys—even if the “square count” looks similar on paper.

Material tier matters

Architectural laminate shingles are common on Missouri homes; designer or impact-rated products cost more. Metal, synthetic underlayment upgrades, and premium ventilation accessories also shift price. Your choice should match code, manufacturer requirements for warranty, and how long you plan to stay in the home.

What is under the shingles

Tear-off sometimes exposes soft decking from long-term leaks. Replacing bad sheets is not optional if you want a sound roof. Ice and water shield in vulnerable valleys may be required depending on code and roof geometry. These items are legitimate line items—not upsells—when conditions warrant them.

Insurance vs. retail

If your project is claim-driven, the carrier’s estimate sets a baseline, but it may not capture everything until tear-off. Retail jobs (no claim) are quoted as a full scope from the start. Either way, get it in writing. For claims context, read how insurance claims work for roof damage.
